    Compliance isn't an add-on.
    It's built into every message.

    • Your business named in every first message
    • Opt-out included in every first message

    • One do-not-contact list across every campaign
    • Honest AI: it says it's an AI assistant if asked, and on every call
    • Calls disclosed as transcribed, up front
    • Sending hours that respect your customers

    FAQ

    Questions, answered

    Is there really nothing upfront?

    Yes, on our performance plan there's no setup fee and no retainer. We add a card when you start, but it's only charged for the anchor points you've reached, weekly, after a 48 hour window to dispute any of them.

    What's an "anchor point"?

    A result we both agree is worth paying for, written down before we start. Most businesses pay when a lead books a call with their team. You can see every anchor point as it happens in your dashboard.

    What if the campaign doesn't work?

    Then it doesn't hit any anchor points, and you don't pay. That's the point of performance pricing: we only make money when we bring you results.

    Is it legal to text old leads?

    In Australia, marketing texts need the person's consent, must identify your business and must let people opt out (the Spam Act 2003). We only text people who enquired with you and agreed to be contacted, and you confirm that for every list. Every first message names your business and how to opt out, and opt-outs are honoured straight away. You're still responsible for your list, so leave out anyone you're unsure about.

    Does the AI pretend to be a person?

    No. If a lead asks, it says it's an AI assistant for your business and that a real person from your team will call. On phone calls it says so at the start.

    What happens when someone replies STOP?

    They get one confirmation and are never texted from our number again. You can also add numbers to the do-not-contact list, or upload your existing one.

    How old can the leads be?

    Leads from the last couple of years usually work best, but it depends on your industry. We'll look at your list in the demo and tell you honestly if it's worth running.

    How is my data handled?

    Your lead list is only used to run your campaigns, and only you (and our team) can see your leads. See our Privacy Policy for the detail, including the providers we use.
